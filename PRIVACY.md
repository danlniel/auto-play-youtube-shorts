# Privacy Policy — Auto Play YouTube Shorts

_Last updated: August 2, 2026_

## Summary

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data. Period.

## Details

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, and makes no network requests of its own.
- **Local settings only.** The single on/off preference is stored via `chrome.storage.sync`, which is managed by your Chrome profile. It never leaves Google's Chrome sync infrastructure and is never visible to the developer.
- **Page access.** The content script runs only on `youtube.com` pages and only observes the playback state of the currently playing Short in order to advance to the next one. Nothing about your viewing activity is recorded or transmitted anywhere.
- **No third parties.** No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.
